About the service

Why Tenancy Support Services Matter

For eligible Montana Medicaid members, Tenancy Support Services may help address barriers to obtaining or maintaining housing. Depending on assessed needs, an authorized service plan, and program requirements, support may include identifying housing barriers, developing a housing plan, assistance with housing searches and applications, communication with landlords, connection to community resources, tenant education, and follow-up related to housing stability.

Montana DPHHS describes these services in its Tenancy Support Services overview and TSS policy. Service availability and authorization depend on applicable eligibility, assessment, planning, and program review. Anchor does not directly provide ongoing rental subsidies or shelter and cannot guarantee housing placement or authorization.
