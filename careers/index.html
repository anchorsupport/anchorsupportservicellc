The opportunity

Housing support with clear next steps

The role helps eligible clients/members acquire, maintain, or stabilize safe and reliable housing through practical planning, follow-up, and coordination.

Housing planning and search

Support housing assessments and plans; identify housing goals, barriers, strengths, preferences, and next steps; assist with housing searches, applications, document gathering, and voucher or waitlist follow-up.

Tenancy support

Assist with landlord/property communication, reasonable-accommodation support where appropriate, move-in planning, tenant education, housing stabilization, retention, and recertification support.

Coordination and follow-through

Coordinate with approved community partners when authorized and appropriate, track follow-up, complete service documentation, and promptly raise safety, eligibility, authorization, documentation, or billing concerns.

Qualifications

Minimum qualifications

Applicants should meet either of these pathways:

Education pathway

Associate degree in human services, social services, public health, or a related field from an accredited college or university.

Experience pathway

High school diploma, GED, or HiSET plus at least one year of relevant work experience as determined by Anchor Support Service LLC.

Relevant experience may include housing navigation, case-management support, social services, peer support, community outreach, property management, behavioral-health support, substance-use service support, homeless services, reentry services, benefits navigation, tenancy support, or related experience.

Preferred qualifications

  • Experience supporting people experiencing homelessness, housing instability, behavioral-health or substance-use recovery needs, reentry barriers, disabilities, or other housing-related barriers.
  • Experience with housing applications, vouchers, leases, landlords/property managers, tenant education, fair-housing concepts, or housing retention.
  • Clear, objective, timely, and accurate service-note writing and respectful professional communication.
  • Ability to use approved business technology and protect confidential information.
  • Reliable transportation or ability to attend community-based appointments when assigned and appropriate.

Schedule and pay

Variable hours and compensation

Work hours depend on client/member appointments, authorized service needs, available assignments, staffing, and company operational needs. Cancellations, no-shows, rescheduling, eligibility or authorization changes, and lack of allowable service need may change available hours. There is no guaranteed minimum paid or billable hours or fixed schedule.

Probationary starting range: $17.00-$18.00 per hour, based on documented relevant experience, education/training, qualifications, and other lawful job-related factors.

The probationary period is twelve (12) months from date of hire. Successful completion includes a performance and compensation review. The current post-probation target range is generally $18.00-$20.00 per hour, depending on performance, demonstrated competency, responsibilities, current business conditions, and company approval. A pay increase is not automatic or guaranteed.

Professional practice

Documentation, safety, and boundaries

Document only services actually provided and actual time. Keep service notes accurate and timely, confirm or document Medicaid eligibility and authorization status as assigned, protect confidential information, use approved systems, maintain professional boundaries, and report errors or safety concerns promptly. Paid work time and Medicaid-billable time are different; billing is limited to allowable services actually provided and documented.

Staff are not expected to place themselves in unsafe situations. This position does not provide legal or medical advice, therapy, substance-use treatment, financial advising, property-management services, Medicaid eligibility determinations, or final authorization/billing decisions unless separately licensed, credentialed, and authorized.

Work environment

Community-based work and approved technology

Services may take place by phone, text, email, video, in a community or public location, a client/member residence, agency office, landlord/property office, approved remote setting, or another approved setting. The role may require approved technology and travel to community locations when assigned. Transportation alone is not the purpose of this role.
