Landlords, Housing Providers, and Community Organizations

Supporting Successful Tenancies and Strengthening Landlord Relationships

Anchor Support Service LLC provides Tenancy Support Services under Montana’s HEART Waiver. We help eligible individuals navigate housing challenges, build tenancy skills, communicate with housing providers, and work toward stable housing.

Contact Anchor How We Help

How We Support Landlords and Tenants

Practical support within each person’s service plan

Support is individualized, subject to program requirements, and based on the client’s goals and authorized services.

Communication Support

Help clients understand housing concerns, prepare for conversations, and plan responses. Direct communication with a landlord requires appropriate client authorization or another valid legal basis.

Eviction Risk Reduction

Help clients respond to lease warnings, understand housing-related concerns, identify possible next steps, and connect with appropriate resources. Anchor cannot stop or guarantee prevention of an eviction.

Lease Education

Help clients understand lease expectations and responsibilities, plan for housing costs, care for their home, and build appropriate neighbor relationships. This is not legal advice.

Housing Stability

Provide individualized follow-up to help clients address housing barriers that may put a tenancy at risk, within the client’s service plan and program requirements.

Community Coordination

Connect clients with housing, legal, financial, behavioral-health, and community resources when appropriate. Each organization determines its own eligibility, availability, and services.

When a Tenancy Is at Risk

Help with practical next steps

An eligible client facing a possible eviction may receive housing-related assistance as allowed by their service plan and program requirements. Support depends on the person’s situation and does not guarantee a particular resolution.

Anchor cannot stop or guarantee prevention of an eviction. For legal advice or representation, contact a qualified legal provider.

  • Communication coaching
  • Help organizing housing notices and paperwork
  • Support contacting a landlord with appropriate authorization
  • Budgeting and housing-retention planning
  • Help exploring possible mutually agreed resolutions
  • Referrals to rental-assistance programs or qualified legal assistance when appropriate

Clear Roles

What Anchor Does Not Provide

  • Anchor does not directly pay ongoing rent or rental arrears.
  • Anchor does not guarantee rental payments or co-sign leases.
  • Anchor does not provide legal advice or legal representation.
  • Anchor is not a landlord, property manager, or enforcement agency.
  • Anchor cannot guarantee tenant compliance or specific housing outcomes.
  • Anchor cannot guarantee financial assistance or housing placement.

Any application-fee or security-deposit assistance, if applicable, depends on specific program authorization. It is not direct financial assistance from Anchor, and funding availability is not promised.

Working With Anchor

Respectful coordination with clear boundaries

Landlords, property managers, housing providers, and community organizations can contact Anchor to discuss general partnership opportunities or concerns affecting an existing tenancy.

Client privacy and housing standards

Client-specific communication requires appropriate client authorization or another valid legal basis. Do not send confidential client information through a website form or ordinary email.

No landlord is required to waive lawful rental standards. Contacting Anchor does not establish a person’s eligibility for services, and Anchor cannot guarantee a tenant will qualify.
